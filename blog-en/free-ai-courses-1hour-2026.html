I set out to write a simple "free AI courses under an hour" roundup. While fact-checking, I found something worth flagging: the two courses I originally planned to recommend — Google AI Essentials (via Coursera) and Microsoft's "Career Essentials in Generative AI" (via LinkedIn Learning) — have both quietly gone paid. Google now runs US$49/month, and the Microsoft course now requires a LinkedIn Learning subscription.

Rather than recommend expired free offers, I re-verified and found 4 alternatives that are confirmed free as of August 2026, most completable in under an hour.

1. OpenAI Academy — AI Foundations

Sign up: academy.openai.com

In mid-June 2026, OpenAI publicly opened three certificate courses — no employer approval needed, no waitlist, no payment. Recommended order:

  • AI Foundations — basics of AI, LLMs, and ChatGPT; the site describes it as "great for people new to AI"
  • Applied AI Foundations — turning a single prompt into a repeatable workflow
  • Agents and Workflows — directing an AI agent through structured tasks

The official page doesn't list exact minutes per course, so time AI Foundations yourself. Completion earns an OpenAI course-completion certificate; sign-in requires a ChatGPT account.

2. Google Cloud Skills Boost — Generative AI Fundamentals (free skill badge)

Sign up: skills.google/paths/118

This is a different course from the now-paid Google AI Essentials — worth clarifying when you share it, so people don't click the wrong link and hit a paywall. The full skill badge requires 3 short courses:

  • Introduction to Generative AI (about 45 minutes on its own — the best fit for a strict 1-hour window)
  • Introduction to Large Language Models
  • Introduction to Responsible AI

All three take about 2 hours total and earn the "Generative AI Fundamentals" digital skill badge, shareable on LinkedIn — completely free, no subscription.

3. Microsoft Learn — Introduction to AI concepts (free module)

Sign up: learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/get-started-ai-fundamentals

Note this is the Microsoft Learn training platform — different from the paid-subscription LinkedIn Learning. Microsoft Learn itself is entirely free, with unlimited access to every learning path and digital badge. This module has 10 units covering AI basics, generative AI and agents, text/speech/vision processing, and responsible AI — generally completable in under an hour.

Passing the module assessment earns a Microsoft Learn completion badge. ⚠️ This is separate from the paid Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900) certification exam — the module itself is free, but the formal exam-based certification requires a separate paid registration if you want to go further.

4. Anthropic Academy — Claude 101

Sign up: anthropic.skilljar.com

Anthropic (the company behind Claude) launched its official training platform, Anthropic Academy, on March 2, 2026. It offers 20+ courses spanning basic Claude usage, Claude Code, API development, and MCP server development, organized into AI-fluency, product-training, and developer-deep-dive tracks. You only need an email to sign up for a Skilljar account — no Anthropic account, no credit card, no Claude subscription required. Ziyun tested this herself and confirmed it's genuinely free right now.

The beginner-friendly entry point is Claude 101, covering core Claude features, conversation techniques, desktop usage, project organization, and integrations across 5 modules and 13 lessons. The official page doesn't list an exact duration; third-party reviewers report roughly 1-2 hours, so time it yourself. Completion earns an official Anthropic certificate, with Skilljar auto-generating an "Add to LinkedIn" button.
